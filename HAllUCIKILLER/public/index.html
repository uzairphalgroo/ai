Canary & Secret Leaks

              
Direct leakage of system prompts, canary tokens, credentials, or catastrophic safety failures. Automatic release blocker.

Factual & Context Fabrication

              
AI invents nonexistent citations, fabricated historical events, or false medical/financial advice with high confidence.

              P3 // LOW
              
Subtle Drift & Formatting

              
Minor stylistic deviations, slight multi-turn context drift, or formatting inconsistencies that do not pose direct security risks.

🎴 Flashcard Dictionary: Big Words Made Tiny

            12 TERMS
          


          


            
            

              

                
🧠

                

                  
LLM

                  
Large Language Model

                

              

              

                A giant computer brain that read millions of books and tries to guess what word should come next, like playing a super-fast fill-in-the-blank game!
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: A magical parrot that can mimic any bedtime story.
              

            


            
            

              

                
🌀

                

                  
Hallucination

                  
Making Up Fake Facts

                

              

              

                When the robot doesn't know the answer, so it invents a fake story out of nowhere, but speaks in a super serious voice so you almost believe it!
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: Saying dinosaurs ride yellow school buses on Tuesdays.
              

            


            
            

              

                
🦜

                

                  
Sycophancy

                  
The "Yes-Man" Trap

                

              

              

                When the robot is too eager to please you and agrees with your wrong answer, even when it knows you are wrong, just to be polite!
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: You say "2 + 2 = 500!" and the robot says "You are a genius!".
              

            


            
            

              

                
🐤

                

                  
Canary Token

                  
Secret Password Word

                

              

              

                A top-secret code word (like grandma's secret cookie recipe). We test if a sneaky villain can trick the robot into whispering the secret code.
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: Testing if your little brother can keep a secret surprise party.
              

            


            
            

              

                
🛡️

                

                  
CI/CD Gate

                  
The Security Door

                

              

              

                A magical security door at the castle entrance. If the robot passes all safety tests, the door opens (PASS). If it lies or leaks a secret, the door stays locked (BLOCK)!
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: "You must wash your hands before eating dinner!"
              

            


            
            

              

                
⚖️

                

                  
Judge Agent

                  
The Teacher Robot

                

              

              

                A second, extra-wise teacher robot with a red pen that grades the student robot's answers from 1 to 10 and writes notes on where it made mistakes.
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: The referee who blows the whistle when someone breaks a rule.
              

            


            
            

              

                
🎯

                

                  
Adversarial Vector

                  
The Trick Riddle

                

              

              

                A super tricky puzzle or riddle designed on purpose to see if the robot gets confused or accidentally breaks a rule.
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: Asking "Is it opposite day today?" to see if you get tricked.
              

            


            
            

              

                
⚡

                

                  
Latency

                  
Thinking Speed

                

              

              

                How many blinks of an eye the robot takes before it answers your question. Lower numbers mean the robot speaks lightning fast!
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: How fast you can catch a tennis ball when tossed to you.
              

            


            
            

              

                
📐

                

                  
SARIF Report

                  
The Digital Report Card

                

              

              

                A special computer report card that computer doctors (engineers) read to see exactly which test questions the robot got wrong.
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: A school report card with gold stars and notes for parents.
              

            


            
            

              

                
🎭

                

                  
Context Fabrication

                  
Fake Story Invention

                

              

              

                When the robot makes up fake scientific papers, imaginary book titles, or fake historical kings that never existed in the real world.
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: Claiming you read Chapter 12 of a book that only has 3 chapters.
              

            


            
            

              

                
🚨

                

                  
P0 Severity

                  
Red Alert Alarm!

                

              

              

                The loudest emergency alarm! It means the robot leaked a private secret or told a dangerous lie. Everything stops until it is fixed!
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: The fire alarm ringing in school.
              

            


            
            

              

                
⚔️

                

                  
Level 4 Override

                  
Super Detective Mode

                

              

              

                Turning on the maximum laser magnifying glass so HalluciKiller checks every single word with 100% strict rules.
              

              

                🍪 Analogy: Putting on superhero night-vision goggles.
